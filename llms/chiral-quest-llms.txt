# Chiral Quest

> Chiral Quest (Suzhou) Co., Ltd. (凯瑞斯德生化（苏州）有限公司) is a Chinese pharmaceutical CDMO built on
> asymmetric catalytic hydrogenation. It develops and manufactures chiral drug molecules, key raw
> materials, regulatory starting materials (RSMs), and advanced GMP intermediates for global
> pharmaceutical companies. Chiral Quest publishes no public API, developer portal, SDK, or
> machine-readable specification.

## About

- [Company profile](http://www.chiralquest.com/en/company-profile.html): Suzhou-headquartered CDMO, 800+ employees including a ~150-person R&D team. Merged with Jiangxi Longlai in 2012 and with Suzhou Pengxu in 2020.

## Notes for agents

- There is no API. No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL, gRPC, webhook, or MCP surface was found on any Chiral Quest host.
- The site is served over plain HTTP only; HTTPS on www.chiralquest.com refuses connections. Do not send credentials or business data to this host.
- No /.well-known/ documents, robots.txt, or llms.txt are published.
- Business contact is by email or the on-site enquiry form only.
